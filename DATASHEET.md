# MSI-Bench Datasheet

Following Gebru et al., *Datasheets for Datasets* (CACM 2021).

## Motivation
A reproducible semi-synthetic benchmark for credibility assessment and fusion of multi-source Maritime Safety Information. No public corpus of crowdsourced MSI exists, and labelled real attacks are unobtainable; the crowdsourced and adversarial layers are therefore simulated by necessity (Section 4.1).

## Provenance & channel separation
The real backbone is the public-domain NGA NAVAREA IV archive. Narrative text in synthetic records is LLM- or template-generated; ALL coordinates, timestamps, device/network identifiers, tampering perturbations, and forged credentials are produced by audited code. Every synthetic record stores its generator, split, and attack provenance.

## Collection process
Generated by `msi_bench.generation.build_benchmark`; lifecycle references are masked from official text where chains define ground truth. Real replay text is drawn from cancelled 2020–2021 warnings.

## Recommended uses
Framework validation under an explicit threat model. NOT a substitute for operational field data. License: NGA broadcast warnings are U.S. government work (public domain); synthetic overlay released under the repository license.
